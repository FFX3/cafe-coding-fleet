### New Editions (Sept 2026)

Salesforce launched Core ($195), Advanced ($395), and Max ($550) - these bundle Agentforce, Slack, analytics, security, and Premier Support.

### Hidden Costs

- **Add-ons**: CPQ, analytics, Agentforce capacity, premium support
- **Implementation**: Often costs as much as first year of licenses
- **Data migration**: Additional cost
- **Consultants/admins**: Ongoing configuration needs
- **Price escalation**: ~6% increase in Aug 2025, expect annual bumps

### Key Quote

> "The license is only the start. Add-ons, integrations, and implementation often cost as much as the seats."

> "A team that budgets from $25 and lands on Enterprise plus Premier Support is off by roughly a factor of eight per seat before implementation."

### Billing

- Starter Suite: monthly billing available
- Everything else: **annual billing, paid upfront**
