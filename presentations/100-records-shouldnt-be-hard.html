Why does this happen?

Visual workflow tools store state for every step.

Just in case you pause. Just in case you debug. Just in case you retry.

100 records × 5 steps × cascading workflows = 100,000+ database entries

Salesforce has the same problem.

They just handle it differently.

  • 100 queries per transaction (then it crashes)
  • 10 seconds CPU time (then it crashes)
  • 150 database operations (then it crashes)

The "fix" requires developer knowledge.

  • Query before the loop, not inside it
  • Collection variables to batch changes
  • Bulkification patterns

That's programming. Just with boxes instead of text.
