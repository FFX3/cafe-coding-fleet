"Well, you know how a lot of business owners get frustrated with getting pulled in to approve tech stuff - a 2FA code, a vendor calling to verify a request, employees waiting on you before they can move forward?

Well, what I do is help companies like that with their marketing, their sales pipeline, and onboarding new hires.

Does that resonate with your company or something you might be experiencing?"

---

ICP CONTEXT (not part of script):
- Not necessarily tech savvy - that's the point
- High revenue, low headcount
- They're the blocker, not by choice
- Tech permission bottleneck: 2FA, vendor verification calls
- Time best spent with clients

KEY INSIGHT:
- I'm not just a tech guy - I fix tech pain to help with business outcomes
- Tech is supposed to solve marketing, sales, onboarding problems
- But mismanagement makes those problems worse
- Example: single CRM seat shared across team → can't do performance reviews, can't track individual work
- They're using enterprise-priced tools to solve small headcount problems
- My solution: OSS tools on their infra, unlimited seats, right-sized for small teams

NOTES:
- "help with X, X, and X" = business outcomes, NOT the tech
- Marketing, sales pipeline, onboarding - the things tech is supposed to enable
