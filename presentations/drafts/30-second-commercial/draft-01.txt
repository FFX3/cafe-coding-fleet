30 SECOND COMMERCIAL - DRAFT 01

---

PAIN POINTS:
- Transitioning to delegation stage, trying to remove hats
- But tech is tangled: emailing passwords, 2FA codes, employees can't do things without you
- Training new employees on systems is a mess
- Multiple vendors all needing the owner to grant permissions
- Tech pain leads to simplified marketing: "we won't build a landing page per ad set, too complicated"
- They think: "if we just hire a few more people, it'll get better"

THE INSIGHT:
- More headcount makes it WORSE, not better
- They're not systematized
- They outgrew the scale where they could personally touch everything
- The bottleneck IS the owner

THE SOLUTION:
- Empower employees to OWN the solutions to their problems
- OWN your systems

---

VERBAL DRAFT v1:

"You know how a lot of business owners hit this point where they're trying to step back - delegate more, remove some hats - but everything runs through them?

Like, employees asking for 2FA codes. Training new hires on five different systems. Vendors needing you to log in and grant permissions.

And because that basic stuff is such a headache, the team starts simplifying. They don't build a new landing page per ad set because 'it's too complicated.' They're leaving money on the table because the systems aren't there.

And the owner thinks: 'If I just hire a few more people, it'll get better.'

But it gets worse. More headcount, same bottleneck. You're not systematized - you just outgrew the scale where you could personally touch everything.

What I do is help companies like that actually systematize - so your employees can OWN the solutions to their problems. OWN the systems. And you get your time back.

Does that sound like something you might be experiencing?"
