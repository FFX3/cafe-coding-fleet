30 SECOND COMMERCIAL - DRAFT 02

---

THE DISTILLED VERSION:

"You know how a lot of business owners hit this point where they're trying to step back - delegate more, hand things off - but somehow everything still runs through them?

What I do is help companies like that actually systematize - so your team can own the systems instead of waiting on you.

Does that sound like something you might be experiencing?"

---

THEN EXPAND WITH FOLLOW-UP SLIDES:

"How does that show up for you?"

(Let them talk, then if needed, prompt with examples:)

Slide: THE BOTTLENECK SYMPTOMS
- Employees asking for passwords, 2FA codes
- Training new hires is chaos - five different logins, nothing documented
- Vendors needing YOU to log in and grant permissions
- You're the help desk for your own company

Slide: THE MARKETING SYMPTOM
- Generic ads to generic pages because "custom is too complicated"
- Or: different ads for different customer profiles...
  ...but they all hit the same sales pipeline
- Prospect gets to the call confused - the framing changed
- You lose sales you should've closed

Slide: THE FALSE SOLUTION
- "If we just hire a few more people, it'll get better"
- More headcount, same bottleneck
- You're not systematized
- You outgrew the scale where you could touch everything yourself

Slide: THE REAL SOLUTION
- Empower your team to OWN the systems
- One place for passwords
- One place for docs and SOPs
- Marketing that's connected to sales
- You step back, it keeps running

---

NOTES:
- 30 second is the hook, not the full pitch
- Let them self-identify with "how does that show up for you?"
- Slides are for when you need to prompt or expand
- "OWN your systems" is still the through-line
